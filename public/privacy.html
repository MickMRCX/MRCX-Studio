1. Information We Collect Directly

Download Empire does not require you to create an account.

We do not directly request or collect personal information such as your:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • postal address;
  • phone number;
  • contacts;
  • photos;
  • precise location;
  • microphone or camera data.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated in a future version of the game.

2. Advertising

Download Empire uses rewarded advertising. You may voluntarily choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Advertising is provided and mediated using services including:

  • Unity LevelPlay;
  • ironSource;
  • Unity Ads;
  • Google AdMob.

These services may process information to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, perform attribution, and improve advertising services.

This information may include:

  • device identifiers;
  • advertising identifiers where permitted;
  • IP address;
  • device and operating system information;
  • application version and device configuration;
  • advertising impressions and interactions;
  • approximate location derived from technical information such as IP address;
  • diagnostic and performance information;
  • fraud prevention and advertising measurement information.

3. Personalized Advertising and Consent

In regions where consent is legally required, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, advertising partners may request your consent before using certain information for personalized advertising.

If you do not consent to personalized advertising, Download Empire may still display non-personalized or contextual rewarded advertisements where permitted.

4. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On supported Apple devices, Download Empire may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) system.

If you allow tracking, advertising providers may be permitted to access the device advertising identifier and use information for advertising measurement or personalized advertising, subject to applicable law and your other privacy choices.

If you select Ask App Not to Track, the game will continue to function and rewarded advertisements may still be available.

5. Analytics and Diagnostics

Download Empire uses Unity services that may collect technical and diagnostic information to help us understand application performance, stability and crashes.

This information may include:

  • crash reports;
  • errors and exceptions;
  • application session information;
  • device and operating system information;
  • performance information;
  • technical identifiers;
  • other diagnostic information.

Download Empire does not currently use custom gameplay analytics events to build individual player profiles.

6. In-App Purchases

Download Empire may offer in-app purchases, including an option to remove advertising requirements.

Purchases are processed by the platform through which you obtained the application, such as the Apple App Store or Google Play.

We do not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.
