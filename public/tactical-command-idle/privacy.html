Privacy Policy — Tactical Command Idle

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tactical Command Idle is a mobile game developed and published by MRCX Studio.

MRCX Studio respects the privacy of its players and is committed to protecting their personal information.

01Information We Collect

Tactical Command Idle uses Unity Analytics to understand gameplay activity and improve the gaming experience.

Depending on the device and service configuration, information may include:

  • Device information and operating system.
  • Installation-specific identifiers.
  • IP address.
  • Gameplay events, such as missions, progression, and interactions.
  • Application usage and performance information.

We do not require players to provide their real name, postal address, or telephone number to play.

02How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Understand gameplay patterns and player progression.
  • Identify technical issues.
  • Improve game balance, usability, and performance.
  • Evaluate and improve game features.

03Analytics Consent

Unity Analytics data collection is subject to the player's privacy choices and applicable laws.

Where consent is required, players must be able to decline analytics collection. Players may also withdraw consent through the privacy controls provided in the game.

04In-App Purchases

Tactical Command Idle offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play and, where available, the Apple App Store.

Payments are processed by the respective platform. MRCX Studio does not directly receive or store credit card numbers or complete payment credentials.

Purchase-related information may be processed by the platform and Unity IAP to validate transactions, deliver purchased content, and support purchase restoration.

05Advertising

Tactical Command Idle does not integrate advertising SDKs and does not display third-party advertisements.

We do not use advertising identifiers for targeted advertising within the game.

06Third-Party Services

The game uses the following services:

  • Unity Technologies — Analytics and In-App Purchasing.
  • Google Play — Application distribution and payment processing.
  • Apple App Store — Application distribution and payment processing, where applicable.

These services process information according to their own privacy policies.

07Data Retention and Security

Data is retained only as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to service-provider retention periods and applicable legal obligations.

We rely on appropriate technical and organizational measures, including those provided by our service providers, to protect information.

08Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to withdraw consent.

You may contact MRCX Studio to exercise these rights.

09Children's Privacy

Tactical Command Idle is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child's personal information has been collected improperly, please contact us.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the game, its services, or applicable regulations.

The latest version will be published on the MRCX Studio website.
